Baked Apples and Oats

A warm, hearty dish of rolled oats baked with sweet honey and tender, caramelized apples. Naturally spiced with cinnamon, this simple baked oatmeal is nourishing, comforting, and full of wholesome, naturally sweet flavors.

20 min. Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 3 apples, peeled and diced
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 1½ cups milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Melt 2 tbsp butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the apples and cook until they begin to soften. Stir in 2 tbsp honey, the cinnamon, and a pinch of salt, cooking until the apples are tender and lightly caramelized. Set aside.
  2. In a bowl, mix the milk, eggs, and remaining 2 tbsp honey. Stir in the oats, remaining salt, 1 tbsp melted butter, and about one-third of the cooked apples.
  3. Grease a baking dish or skillet with butter and pour in half of the oat mixture. Spread the remaining cooked apples over the top, then pour the rest of the oats over the apples for a layered effect.
  4. Bake until the oats are firm in the center and lightly golden on top, about 25–30 minutes.
  5. Let rest briefly, then serve topped with any remaining warm apples or a drizzle of honey.
