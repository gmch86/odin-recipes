Skyr with Honey and Nuts

Creamy Icelandic skyr topped with a drizzle of golden honey and a sprinkle of crunchy nuts. This wholesome treat is high in protein, naturally smooth, and perfect for breakfast, a snack, or a light dessert.

10 min. Serves 1

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup skyr
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1/4 cup mixed nuts

Instructions

  1. Scoop the skyr into a serving bowl.
  2. Sprinkle in the mixed nuts.
  3. Drizzle honey over the top.
  4. Mix everything together and serve immediately!
