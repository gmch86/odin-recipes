Creamy Potato and Mushroom Soup

Home

Comforting, hearty, and packed with wholesome goodness, this soup is perfect for cozy days. Made with golden potatoes, onions, carrots, and baby bella mushrooms, it features a rich, creamy broth and deep, earthy flavors.

Ingredients

  • 1–1.5 lbs gold potatoes, diced
  • 15 oz baby bella mushrooms, diced
  • 1 lb carrots, peeled and sliced
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 3.5–4 cups vegetable broth
  • 4–6 cloves garlic, minced
  • ½ cup canned coconut milk
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1.5 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1–1.5 tsp dried thyme
  • Additional salt and fresh cracked black pepper, to taste
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tbsp reduced sodium soy sauce (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat a large pot over medium-high heat and add the oil. Once hot, add the mushrooms, carrots, and onions. Cook until the mushrooms shrink and the carrots are tender. Optional: cook the mushrooms separately first, then cook the carrots and onions to get extra browning.
  2. Stir in the minced garlic, rosemary, and thyme (you can also add salt and pepper now). Cook for about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add the diced potatoes, then pour in the vegetable broth. Stir to combine.
  4. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce heat to medium-low. Cook for at least 15 minutes, or until the potatoes are soft. For quicker cooking, use medium heat and check frequently with a fork.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk and soy sauce. Taste and adjust salt and pepper as needed before serving.
