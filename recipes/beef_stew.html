Beef Stew

Tender meat and earthy root vegetables simmer together in this rich, flavorful stew. Warm and satisfying, it’s the perfect dish for a cold night or to fuel up after a long day of adventure.

2 hrs Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef or venison
  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 2 parsnips
  • 2 turnips
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the onions and cook until softened.
  2. Add the meat and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Stir in the carrots, parsnips, turnips, salt, pepper, thyme, sage, and rosemary.
  4. Pour in the beef broth, bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for 1-2 hours, or until the meat is tender and the vegetables are cooked through.
  5. Serve hot with a side of flatbread or rustic rye bread.
