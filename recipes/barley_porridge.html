Barley Porridge

A warm, nourishing bowl of barley mixed with sweet honey and crunchy toasted nuts. Topped with fresh or dried berries, this simple porridge is hearty, wholesome, and full of natural flavors.

20 min. Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked barley
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1/4 cup mixed nuts
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • Fresh or dried berries

Instructions

  1. Warm the cooked barley with buttermilk in a small pot over low heat.
  2. Stir in the honey until evenly mixed.
  3. Chop the nuts and sprinkle them over the porridge.
  4. Top with fresh or dried berries.
  5. Drizzle on more honey as needed and enjoy a wholesome, filling meal.
